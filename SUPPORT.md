## Frequently asked questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the **Profile** tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap **Delete account**. You'll be asked to re-confirm with Sign in with Apple. Once confirmed, your account, leaderboard memberships, and personal stats are removed.

### How do I join a friend's leaderboard?

On the home screen, tap **Create**, then **Join Board**, and enter the 6-character invite code your friend shared with you.

### How do I create a new leaderboard?

On the home screen, tap **Create**, then **New Board**. Pick a name, pick a game, choose a point system, and start logging matches. The 6-character invite code is generated for you to share.

### Can I edit a match after I submit it?

Not in v1.0. If you logged a match incorrectly, contact support and I'll help you fix it.

### Why is my rank not changing the way I expect?

Ranks update based on match placement and the active point system. Some leaderboards use a top-3-only system, which awards points only to the top 3 finishers. Open the leaderboard and tap the gear icon to see the active point system.

### Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) and is only visible to you and the members of leaderboards you join. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

### What devices does the app support?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.5 or later.
